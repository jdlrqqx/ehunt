## The Features That Actually Make a Difference

**Product Research** is the feature most sellers start with. You can filter through millions of Etsy listings by sales volume, weekly trends, price range, favorites count, and category. More usefully, EHunt shows historical data across four dimensions — sales, price, favorites, and reviews — so you can tell whether a product is on an upswing or starting to fade. There's even data on Amazon Handmade listings and inactive Etsy products, which is handy for spotting niches other sellers have quietly abandoned.

**Keyword Research** gives you access to over 14 million keyword terms, updated daily. You can run batch analysis on up to 100 keywords at once, which alone saves hours compared to checking them one at a time. Search volume, competition levels, long-tail variations, and seasonal peaks are all in there. For anyone trying to rank Etsy listings, this is where most of the time gets spent — and rightly so.

**Shop Analyzer** lets you look inside any Etsy shop. Top products, tags, sales estimates, recent additions and removals, review patterns. It's the kind of competitive intelligence that used to require hours of manual checking. EHunt surfaces it in seconds.

**Ad Analysis** is a feature that most competitors simply don't have. EHunt tracks 2.3 million Etsy ads — which products are being promoted, what strategies top sellers are running, and which niches are getting ad spend. If you're planning to run ads or just want to know where the competition is investing, this data is hard to find anywhere else.

**Real-Time Competitor Tracking** lets you monitor specific shops, products, or keywords with daily updates. Pricing shifts, new arrivals, keyword ranking changes — you'll see them as they happen rather than discovering them weeks later.

**AI-Powered Shop Operation** is the newer side of EHunt, and it's growing fast. The platform now includes an AI title and description generator, an AI image editor for background removal and replacement, an AI Pattern Extractor that reverse-engineers bestselling designs so you can create similar-but-original versions, and an AI Mockup Generator that automatically applies patterns to product photos. The listing optimization tool also syncs directly to your Etsy shop, which cuts out a tedious manual step.

**Review Follow-Up / Email Marketing** connects to your Etsy shop and sends AI-assisted review request messages with intelligent order filtering. For sellers who rely on reviews for social proof, this quietly does a job that most people either forget or dread.

The three tools that come up most often in comparison are Everbee, eRank, and Alura.

Against **eRank**, the trade-off is more nuanced. eRank has a well-established community and solid Google keyword integration. EHunt has a cleaner interface, a much broader product database, ad research capabilities, and AI tools eRank doesn't offer. If you value community-plus-depth, eRank has its merits. If you want breadth, AI features, and a lower price, EHunt is the easier call.

Against **Alura**, EHunt is generally more affordable and covers more Etsy-specific territory, especially on the product research and ad analysis side.
